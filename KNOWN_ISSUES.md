# v0.1.0-beta known issues / scope

- Initial beta target: Skyrim 1.7.104.0 + SKSE 2.3.1 on Steam.
- Initial DOOM target: Steam DOOM + DOOM II / AppID 2280 layout.
- Tiny alpha pinholes may occasionally appear in parts of the first-person
  DOOM weapon overlay.
- Blood/gore presentation currently relies heavily on Skyrim-native impact
  effects and is more restrained than DOOM.
- Knockback can vary for unusually large or non-standard Skyrim creatures.
- All seven current SkyDoom weapon slots are available from the start.
- Controller-specific mapping is not a primary focus of this beta.
- Enhanced soundtrack integration is being considered for a future/full
  release and is not part of v0.1.0-beta.
